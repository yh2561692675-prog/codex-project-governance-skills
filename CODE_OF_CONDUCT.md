# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want a respectful, technically rigorous community where people can ask questions, report failures, and disagree without harassment or discrimination.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, specific, and patient.
- Assume good intent while checking evidence.
- Credit prior work and respect licenses.
- Protect private information and security reports.
- Prefer reproducible facts over status theater or personal attacks.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate exposure of secrets, impersonation, spam, and knowingly misleading claims about tests, adoption, approval, or release are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately through the security or repository contact channel. Maintainers may edit, close, or remove content and may restrict participation when needed to protect contributors or users. Decisions should be proportionate, documented, and consistent.
